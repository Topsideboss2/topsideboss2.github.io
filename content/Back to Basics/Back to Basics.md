> [!tip] One Principle: ***Keep It Stupid Simple***

There is a certain kind of engineering confidence that comes not from knowing the most advanced tools, but from having an unusually solid grip on the fundamentals. 

This section is built on that belief.

**Back to Basics** is where I slow down and explain things from the ground up, no assumed knowledge, no skipped steps, no hand-waving over the parts that are "obvious." The goal is the kind of explanation you would give to someone smart who has never seen this before: precise, honest about complexity, and useful, long after you have forgotten the tutorial you learned it from.

The name comes from the KISS principle: *Keep It Stupid Simple*. Not because the topics are simple, but because clarity is a discipline. The best explanations are the ones that make you wonder why it ever seemed complicated.
